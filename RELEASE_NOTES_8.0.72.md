## SympResHost 8.0.72 — first public release

**Bring your VST / AU piano to life — and to realism.**

### Download
- `SympResHost-8.0.72-macOS-AppleSilicon.pkg` — signed and notarized installer
  (Audio Unit, VST3, Standalone)
- SHA-256: `c0179152bce2bd5e14ee70a4e955edd8d8391c74d18333efa39004619baf20ab`

### Requirements
Apple Silicon Mac (M2 or later recommended), macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Intel / Rosetta not supported.

### Highlights
- Physically modelled sympathetic resonance and sustain, built from the measured partials of the hosted piano
- Continuous half-pedalling, sostenuto, pedal catch (pedal pressed after the notes)
- Natural string release when the dampers fall
- Microphone-pair stereo phase for a wider, deeper image
- Built-in Steinway D-274 model, or capture your own piano
- Live damper mechanics view

### Before you play
In the hosted piano, turn off its own sympathetic resonance and sustain samples/resonance
(keep half-pedal options). With VSL pianos, enable **Force Sustain Samples Off**.
See the README for tested pianos (VSL Synchron Steinway D-274, Ivory 3, Kontakt).
